SMALL FOOTPRINT. GOOD INTENTIONS.

Made for the moment.

Play music in a website, then click the Boreal toolbar icon. Boreal listens only to that tab for your selected duration. You can close the popup while it listens.

Rust + WebAssembly creates the fingerprint on your device. Only the fingerprint is sent to Shazam, which also receives your IP address. Raw audio is discarded. Cover images load from Shazam or Apple when shown. Your latest 2,000 successful discoveries and preferences stay in this browser profile; CSV exports include source page titles and URLs.

Recognition uses Shazam’s unofficial endpoint with no API key, subscription, or hosted backend. Internet access is required; availability and rate limits can change. Browser internal pages, local files, and some protected media cannot be captured. Not affiliated with Shazam or Apple.

Built with the open-source SongRec fingerprinting engine. GPL-3.0-or-later. SongRec ↗ · Attributions ↗
